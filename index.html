01 Numbness
one distant window still flickers, faintly and unevenly. Look around and click it. Hints appear if you need them.
02 Fracture
Click four highlighted windows. Each click makes one light permanent.
03 Awakening
Click five outlined dark buildings. Light travels to them automatically.
04 Reclaiming Self
Connect five pairs of golden building points. Drag between two points, or click one and then another.
05 Afterglow
you fly her. Mouse or ← → to steer, ↓ to descend, hold ↑ to rise toward the light; let go to hover. Cross the light to finish.
Voice
loud sounds make the broken city tremble; a steady sound calms the rain; pitch colours the light. Optional.
