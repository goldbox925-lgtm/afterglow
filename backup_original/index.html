Find the pulse
drag (or arrows) to look around, wheel to zoom, click the pulsing window. Hints appear if you need them; Enter works when it is near the centre.
Bring it back
click dark towers (five) — or press and hold on one, or point and hold a steady sound.
The flight
move the mouse, or use the arrow keys / WASD, to steer her between the towers.
Voice
loud sounds make the broken city tremble; a steady sound helps the light travel; pitch colours it. Optional.
